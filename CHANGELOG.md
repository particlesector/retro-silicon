# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and component versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## Unreleased

### Added

- `common/crc32`: CRC-32 (IEEE 802.3, as zlib), one byte per enabled clock.
- `common/uart`: 8N1 receiver and transmitter; back-to-back bytes take exactly 10 bit times.
- `common/fifo`: synchronous FIFO with a registered read port, inferred as block RAM.
- `common/por`: power-on reset.
- `systems/uart-echo`: UART link test, with a Tang Nano 20K build at 1.5 Mbaud.
- `harness`: link interface, bit-level UART models, CRC32, the echo test and its Verilator backend.
- CMake helpers, FuseSoC manifests for every component, and lint tests (Verilator, Yosys, FuseSoC).
- `debug/test-shell`: UART command engine with CRC-checked frames, retries, build ID, PING, INFO, PEEK and POKE.
- `systems/shell-ram`: the test shell with a scratch RAM, with a Tang Nano 20K build.
- `harness`: test-shell client, `FaultyLink` for fault injection, the shell test, and `SimLink` for any Verilated UART design.
- `testroms/`: manifest of NES test ROMs with SHA-256 sums and expected results, and a fetch script.
- `tools/mesen`: MesenCE Lua runner and checker for blargg-style test ROMs.
- `rs_build_id`: a build ID derived from a system's RTL.
- `cpu/6502`: NMOS 6502, cycle-exact on the bus, with the stable unofficial opcodes and optional NMOS decimal mode; checked against perfect6502 in lockstep, nestest and blargg's instr_test.
- `cmake/Perfect6502.cmake`: fetches perfect6502 at a pinned commit, with SHA-256 checks, for the CPU's lockstep test.
- `memory/sdram`: byte-port controller for a 2M x 32 SDR SDRAM such as the Tang Nano 20K's, with a timing-checking chip model for sim tests.
- `systems/shell-sdram`: the test shell with the SDRAM, with a Tang Nano 20K build and the harness's SDRAM test.
- `systems/nes`: the CPU with RAM, PRG RAM and NROM PRG from SDRAM, run control, a reset-vector override, a stop-at-cycle register and a CRC of the CPU's bus; a Tang Nano 20K build; nestest passes on it with the CPU bench's trace CRC.
- `harness`: iNES parsing, the NES console client and nestest runner, and optional host timing jitter in `SimLink`.
- `debug/test-shell`: CRC command (protocol version 2), so the host can check an upload without reading it back; `Client::crc`, and `write` and `read` for any length.
- `video/ppu-2c02`: the NES's 2C02 PPU (NTSC), dot-exact, with MesenCE's timing, and a save-state port.
- `common/clock-enable`: a clock enable at NUM/DEN of the clock rate, exact on average.
- `debug/video-probe`: frame and line CRCs of a video stream, and line-by-line capture that holds the system.
- `cpu/6502`: a save-state port; `docs/conventions.md` describes the port every component with state gets.
- `systems/nes`: the PPU, OAM DMA, CHR ROM or RAM and nametable mirroring; the NTSC dot rate from 27 MHz with MesenCE's CPU/PPU alignment; stop at a frame count; frame and line CRCs; frame capture; CPU and PPU save-state access.
- `harness`: `nes::rom_test` and the golden frame CRCs from MesenCE (`golden/nes_frames_crc32.txt`) for 42 PPU test ROMs; CHR upload, frame CRCs and frame capture in `nes::Console`.
- `testroms/`: 40 more PPU test ROMs, and the `zeropage` and `frame` checks.
- `tools/mesen/frames.py`: golden frame CRCs and frame dumps from MesenCE; `tools/frame.py`: frame to PNG and frame diff.
- `memory/arbiter`: fixed-priority sharing of one byte-port memory among N clients.
- `memory/bram`: block RAM behind `memory/sdram`'s byte port.
- `common/save-state`: saves and loads a system's state by walking byte regions of its bus into a buffer.
- `common/crc32`: a save-state write, so a restored CRC carries on.
- `systems/nes`: save states (save at the next frame boundary, load, and the buffer through the shell), the system's own state words, and an unmirrored view of the nametable RAM; `nes_sdram` and `nes_bram` put memory behind it.
- `boards/tang-console-138k`: the Tang Console 138K, at 50 MHz, with uart-echo, shell-ram and the NES on block RAM.
- `harness`: `nes::Console::save_state` and `load_state`, `nes::state_test`, and the NES model on block RAM at 50 MHz (`make_nes_bram_sim`); `SimLink` takes the model's clock rate.
- `video/hdmi`: video timing and TMDS encoding for an HDMI output in DVI mode, 1280x720 at 60 Hz by default, with a C++ TMDS encoder and receiver model for tests.
- `video/line-scaler`: a source's frames on a display of another clock, scaled by a whole number through a ring of lines in block RAM, with the source kept in step with the display.
- `common/cdc`: a flip-flop synchronizer for signals from another clock domain.
- `video/ppu-2c02`: `ppu_2c02_rgb`, the 2C02's 512 colours as RGB, generated by `tools/gen_rgb.py` from a simulation of its composite signal.
- `common/crc32`: `W`, the bits taken each clock (8 by default).
- `systems/nes`: HDMI at 720p (`nes_video`): the picture three times its size, the system keeping pace with the display by waiting at each frame's end, and a CRC of a display frame's TMDS symbols for the shell (registers `3C`-`4D`, status bits 6 and 7).
- `boards/tang-nano-20k`: the NES with HDMI, its clocks from the PLL: 371.25 MHz for the serializers, 74.25 MHz for the pixels and 46.40625 MHz for the system.
- `boards/gowin`: `tmds_phy` (OSER10 serializers and LVDS outputs); `build.sh -k` constrains a clock the design makes; stand-ins for Gowin primitives for the lint tests, through `rs_component(... LINT ...)`.
- `harness`: `nes::hdmi_test`, the video registers in `nes::Console`, `make_nes_hdmi_sim` (the Tang Nano 20K's NES with both its clocks, decoded by a TMDS receiver in the tests), and golden video CRCs (`golden/nes_hdmi_crc32.txt`).
- `boards/pynq-z2`, the first board outside Gowin's family: a Zynq-7020 built with openXC7 through `boards/xilinx/build.sh`, with the UART echo, the test shell, and the NES on block RAM with HDMI at 720p; `rs_board_bitstream` takes `VENDOR xilinx`.
- `boards/xilinx`: `tmds_phy_xc7` (OSERDESE2 serializers and OBUFDS outputs), with a sim test of its channel order, and stand-ins for Xilinx primitives for the lint and sim tests.
- `testroms/`: 39 APU and timing ROMs, the NROM single-test versions of blargg's apu_test, cpu_interrupts_v2, instr_misc and instr_timing, his 2005 APU tests, sprdma_and_dmc_dma and apu_mixer, each confirmed to pass in MesenCE.
- `audio/apu-2a03`: the NES's 2A03 APU (NTSC), cycle-exact: pulse, triangle, noise and DMC channels and the frame counter, with a save-state port and a C++ model it matches cycle for cycle.
- `audio/apu-2a03`: `apu_mixer`, the 2A03's nonlinear mix in two tables, generated by `tools/gen_mixer.py`.
- `video/hdmi`: `AUDIO` mode: HDMI with data islands, 48 kHz stereo L-PCM, audio clock regeneration and the AVI and audio InfoFrames; `packet` flags packet symbols. `tmds_model.hpp` gains an HDMI sink's decoder that checks every packet.
- `systems/nes`: the sound as 48 kHz PCM through `apu_mixer` and `audio/resampler`, out over HDMI; the PCM CRC and count (registers `54` and `58`); the video CRC leaves the islands' packets out.
- `cpu/6502`, `video/ppu-2c02`, `audio/apu-2a03`, `audio/resampler`, `systems/nes`: a `SAVE_STATE` parameter (default 1); 0 leaves out the save-state port's logic for a board short of logic. The Tang Nano 20K's NES builds with it off.
- `audio/resampler`: an audio level to signed 16-bit PCM at a lower rate (low-pass, decimation, DC block), in integer arithmetic, with a save-state port.
- `systems/nes`: the APU, its IRQ and `$4015`; one DMA unit for the DMC's and OAM's DMA, as MesenCE runs them; the APU's 17 state words and the audio CRC (register `50`) in save states, now 20,992 bytes; the channel levels on `a_` ports.
- `boards/gowin/build.sh -s` and `rs_board_bitstream(... SEED)`: a fixed placement seed. nextpnr doesn't check hold for Gowin parts, and some Tang Nano 20K placements that meet timing fail on the chip (with HDMI audio, seeds 2 and 3); its NES builds with seed 5.
- `harness`: the audio CRC (`Console::audio_crc`, `TestResult::audio_crc32`), checked by the save-state test and against `golden/nes_audio_crc32.txt`; 34 APU and timing ROMs in the sim tests.

### Changed

- The Yosys lint stops before technology mapping and adds Yosys's design checks, so it runs in under a second.
- `systems/nes` takes its memory as a byte port, through `memory/arbiter`, instead of driving the SDRAM itself; its `FREQ_MHZ` moved to `nes_sdram`.
- `video/ppu-2c02` registers its next dot every clock and commits it on `ce`, so its dot logic has the clocks between dots: `ce` must now come at least 2 clocks apart, with `vrdata` and the register and state writes settled the clock before. `systems/nes` gives the PPU its video memory for the two clocks before each dot. The Tang Console 138K's NES build goes from 49 MHz to 65 MHz.
- `boards/gowin/build.sh` holds the Gowin open-flow build; each board's `build.sh` calls it with its part. `rs_board_bitstream` replaces `rs_tang_bitstream`.
- `boards/tang-nano-20k`: the UART moved from header pins 41/42 to 71 (FPGA RX) and 72 (FPGA TX), out of the HDMI pairs' banks; the NES runs at 46.40625 MHz (31 clocks a bit).
- `README.md` is shorter and points at the website, retro-silicon.particlesector.com, which generates every component's status from the simulation and board runs; the hand-kept status table is gone.

### Fixed

- `video/line-scaler`: the ring is in memories of 8 bits or fewer, so no toolchain has to use block RAM parity bits; with openXC7 a 9-bit ring lost its ninth bit (the NES's emphasis) on a PYNQ-Z2.
- `memory/sdram`: a WRITE waits the whole of tRAS after its row opens. Its auto-precharge can start under a clock after it, so from about 40 MHz the precharge could come before tRAS.
